    📚 Your Complete 5-Pattern Mastery Path

    This bundle takes you from beginner to intermediate. Master cables, lace, color work, and garment construction!

    PROJECT 1: Colorwork Mitts (4-6 hours)

    Skill Level: Intermediate Beginner

    What you'll master:

    • ✅ Knitting with two colors simultaneously (stranding)
    • ✅ Managing multiple yarn balls without tangling
    • ✅ Reading colorwork charts
    • ✅ Thumb gusset shaping
    • ✅ Creating professional-looking colorwork

    Step-by-step process:

    1. Cast on 40 stitches in main color, join in round
    2. Work ribbed cuff (K2P2) for 2 inches
    3. Begin colorwork chart - introduce second color
    4. Create thumb gusset using M1 increases
    5. Place thumb stitches on holder, complete hand
    6. Pick up thumb stitches, complete in the round

    🎥 Video guide: Managing two colors, reading charts, common colorwork mistakes

    Technique focus:

    • 🎨 Color dominance: Which color appears larger and why it matters
    • 📏 Float management: Keeping yarn floats loose enough to maintain stretch
    • 🧵 Weaving in colorwork ends: Invisible finishing techniques

    PROJECT 2: Cable Knit Socks (6-8 hours)

    Skill Level: Intermediate

    What you'll master:

    • ✅ Cable stitches (easier than they look!)
    • ✅ Sock construction (top-down method)
    • ✅ Heel flap and gusset technique
    • ✅ Kitchener stitch (grafting toes)
    • ✅ Small circumference circular knitting

    Step-by-step process:

    1. Cuff: Cast on 64 stitches, K2P2 rib for 1.5 inches
    2. Leg: Introduce cable pattern (C4F, C4B), work 6-7 inches
    3. Heel flap: Work back and forth on half stitches for 2.5 inches
    4. Turn heel: Follow short-row shaping pattern
    5. Gusset: Pick up stitches along heel flap, decrease to original count
    6. Foot: Continue cable on top, stockinette on bottom
    7. Toe: Simple decreases to 12 stitches, graft with Kitchener

    🎥 Video guide: Complete sock walkthrough, cable techniques, Kitchener stitch demonstration

    New skills explained:

    • 🔀 C4F & C4B cables: Creating rope-like textures without a cable needle
    • 👠 Heel turns: The geometry of heel shaping demystified
    • 🧷 Kitchener stitch: Invisible grafting for seamless toes

    PROJECT 3: Lace Shawl (10-12 hours)

    Skill Level: Intermediate+

    What you'll master:

    • ✅ Lace knitting (yarn overs and decreases)
    • ✅ Reading complex lace charts
    • ✅ Lifelines for complex patterns
    • ✅ Blocking to reveal lace beauty
    • ✅ Working with fine yarn on larger needles

    Step-by-step process:

    1. Cast on 5 stitches (center start)
    2. Work increase section - shawl grows from center out
    3. Introduce lace pattern (yo, k2tog, ssk combinations)
    4. Use lifelines every 10 rows for safety
    5. Work border pattern for elegant edge
    6. Bind off loosely using larger needle
    7. Block aggressively to open up lace holes

    🎥 Video guide: Lace techniques, reading charts, blocking tutorial

    Critical techniques:

    • 🕸️ Yarn overs: Creating intentional holes for lace patterns
    • 📌 Lifelines: Running waste yarn through stitches as safety net
    • 💧 Wet blocking: Transforming crumpled lace into elegant fabric
    • 📖 Chart reading: Following symbol-based lace patterns

    PROJECT 4: Textured Sweater (12-15 hours)

    Skill Level: Intermediate

    What you'll master:

    • ✅ Garment shaping (increases and decreases for fit)
    • ✅ Following sweater schematics
    • ✅ Seaming pieces together
    • ✅ Set-in sleeve construction
    • ✅ Custom sizing adjustments

    Construction method: Bottom-up, pieces seamed

    1. Back: Cast on for your size, work in textured pattern, shape armholes
    2. Front: Same as back, add neck shaping
    3. Sleeves (2): Bottom-up with increases, cap shaping
    4. Seaming: Mattress stitch for invisible side seams
    5. Finishing: Pick up for neckband, weave ends

    🎥 Video guide: Reading schematics, seaming techniques, sizing adjustments

    Fit customization:

    • 📐 Measuring yourself: Bust, length, sleeve length, shoulder width
    • 📊 Choosing size: Ease allowances and fit preferences
    • ✂️ Length adjustments: Adding or removing rows proportionally
    • 🧵 Seaming: Mattress stitch for professional-looking garments

    PROJECT 5: Designer Cowl (6-8 hours)

    Skill Level: Intermediate

    What you'll master:

    • ✅ Combining multiple techniques in one project
    • ✅ Color placement and design choices
    • ✅ Creating your own color combinations
    • ✅ Short row shaping for asymmetry
    • ✅ Design confidence!

    Step-by-step process:

    1. Choose 3-4 coordinating colors from your stash
    2. Cast on for cowl width (pattern provides base structure)
    3. Follow colorwork chart OR create your own stripes
    4. Use short rows to create asymmetrical drape
    5. Join ends with Kitchener or three-needle bind-off

    🎥 Video guide: Color theory basics, short rows, design modifications

    Design elements:

    • 🎨 Color selection: Complementary vs. analogous color schemes
    • 🔄 Short rows: Creating curves and shaping without increases/decreases
    • ✨ Personal style: Making design choices that reflect YOU

    🎯 8-Week Mastery Plan:

    • Weeks 1-2: Colorwork Mitts (learn stranding)
    • Weeks 3-4: Cable Socks (master cables + sock construction)
    • Weeks 5-6: Lace Shawl (patience + blocking skills)
    • Weeks 7-8: Start Sweater (biggest project!)
    • Week 9+: Complete sweater, then celebratory Designer Cowl
    • Total commitment: 40-50 hours over 2-3 months. Achievable!
