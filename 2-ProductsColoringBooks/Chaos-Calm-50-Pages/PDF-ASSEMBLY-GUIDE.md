## 📄 PAGE 5 - 4 THERAPEUTIC TECHNIQUES

**Background:** Soft texture (subtle paper or watercolor effect)

**Text Content:**
```
4 THERAPEUTIC TECHNIQUES

These aren't rules—they're invitations.
Try them when you need structure, skip them when you don't.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

TECHNIQUE 1: GRADIENT BREATHING
Breathe in rhythm with your coloring.

How to practice:
1. Choose two colors (one chaos, one calm)
2. Inhale for 4 counts—press firmly with chaos color
3. Hold for 4 counts—pause, observe the mark
4. Exhale for 4 counts—soften pressure, blend to calm color
5. Repeat across the page

This syncs your breath with your hands.
The page becomes a record of your nervous system settling.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

TECHNIQUE 2: CONTRAST THERAPY
Honor opposing feelings simultaneously.

How to practice:
1. Divide page mentally (left/right or top/bottom)
2. One side: Use only chaos colors (what's hard right now)
3. Other side: Use only calm colors (what you need)
4. Color to the edges—don't let them mix yet
5. Final touch: Add one color that bridges both sides

Sometimes you're both exhausted AND determined.
Both grieving AND grateful. Let the page hold both truths.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

TECHNIQUE 3: REPETITIVE RITUAL
Use pattern to soothe an anxious mind.

How to practice:
1. Choose one simple motion (circular strokes, back-and-forth, dots)
2. Pick one color
3. Repeat the motion over and over, filling space
4. Let your mind go quiet as your hand works
5. Change colors only when it feels right

This isn't about the result. It's about the rhythm.
The motion becomes meditation.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

TECHNIQUE 4: PRESSURE RELEASE
Your grip tells the truth.

How to practice:
1. Notice: Am I holding the pencil tightly? (Chaos mode)
2. If yes: Use heavy pressure, dark colors, let it out
3. As you color: Gradually loosen your grip
4. Watch the colors lighten as you release tension
5. End with barely-there strokes, soft colors

Your hand will teach you how to let go.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Remember: These are tools, not tests.
You're already doing it right.
```

**Design Notes:**
- Technique number badges (1, 2, 3, 4)
- "How to practice" in smaller, list format
- Italicized reflection sentences
- Plenty of white space (not overwhelming)

50 pages. 50 days. One intentional journey.

Focus: Invitation to Peace
• Introduce calm colors (blues, purples, greens)

WEEK 5-6: INTEGRATION (Days 29-42)
Focus: Both/And Balance
• Mix chaos and calm colors on same page
• Let them coexist without blending completely

WEEK 7: RAINBOW LIBERATION (Days 43-50)
Focus: Full Spectrum Freedom
• Use ANY colors that call to you
