## IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

**Email Service Recommendations:**
- ConvertKit (best for creators)
- Mailchimp (free tier available)
- SendGrid (developer-friendly)

**Personalization Tokens:**
- [Name] = User's first name
- [Bundle Name] = Purchased bundle
- [OrderID] = Unique order identifier

**A/B Testing:**
- Test subject lines on Day 5 email (highest conversion)
- Test CTA buttons vs. text links
- Test testimonial placement

**Metrics to Track:**
- Open rate (target: >30%)
- Click rate (target: >5%)
- Conversion rate (target: >2%)
- Unsubscribe rate (keep <0.5%)
