    📚 Your Complete Learning Roadmap (3 Patterns)

    This bundle takes you from absolute beginner to confident knitter. Follow this exact path for best results!

    PROJECT 1: Simple Dishcloth (1-2 hours)

    Skill Level: Absolute Beginner

    What you'll master:

    • ✅ Long-tail cast-on (the most versatile cast-on method)
    • ✅ Knit stitch (the foundation of all knitting)
    • ✅ Purl stitch (the second essential stitch)
    • ✅ Basic bind-off (finishing your work)
    • ✅ Garter stitch pattern (all knit rows)

    Step-by-step process:

    1. Cast on 35 stitches using long-tail method
    2. Knit every stitch, every row until square measures 9 inches
    3. Bind off all stitches loosely
    4. Weave in ends using yarn needle

    🎥 Video guide included: Watch along as we knit the entire dishcloth in real-time

    Time breakdown:

    • Setup & cast-on: 10 minutes
    • Knitting rows: 45-60 minutes
    • Binding off & finishing: 10 minutes

    PROJECT 2: Garter Stitch Scarf (3-5 hours)

    Skill Level: Beginner

    What you'll master:

    • ✅ Maintaining consistent tension over long projects
    • ✅ Counting rows accurately
    • ✅ Joining new balls of yarn mid-project
    • ✅ Creating even edges
    • ✅ Building confidence with repetitive knitting

    Step-by-step process:

    1. Choose your yarn (worsted weight, 2 skeins)
    2. Cast on 30 stitches
    3. Knit every row until scarf measures 60 inches (or desired length)
    4. Bind off loosely
    5. Weave in ends and add optional fringe

    🎥 Video guide included: How to join new yarn, maintain tension, and add fringe

    Why this project matters:

    • 💡 Teaches patience and rhythm
    • 💡 You'll wear your creation immediately!
    • 💡 Perfect gift for friends and family
    • 💡 Builds muscle memory through repetition

    PROJECT 3: Basic Beanie Hat (4-6 hours)

    Skill Level: Beginner+

    What you'll master:

    • ✅ Knitting in the round using circular needles
    • ✅ Understanding gauge and why it matters
    • ✅ Simple decreases for shaping
    • ✅ Creating a stretchy ribbed brim
    • ✅ Your first wearable fitted garment!

    Step-by-step process:

    1. Gauge swatch: Knit a 4x4 inch square to check your tension (explained in detail)
    2. Ribbed brim: Cast on 80 stitches, join in round, work K2P2 rib for 2 inches
    3. Body: Switch to stockinette stitch, knit until hat measures 7 inches from cast-on
    4. Crown decreases: Follow our simple decrease pattern (8 sections, decrease every other round)
    5. Finishing: Cut yarn, thread through remaining stitches, pull tight, weave in ends

    🎥 Video guide included: Joining in the round, placing markers, and decrease techniques

    Supplies you'll need (not included):

    • 16-inch circular needles, US 7 or 8
    • 2 skeins worsted weight yarn
    • Stitch markers
    • Yarn needle

    🎯 4-Week Success Plan:

    • Week 1: Complete dishcloth + watch all video guides
    • Week 2: Knit scarf (20-30 mins/day)
    • Week 3: Buy circular needles, practice gauge swatch
    • Week 4: Knit your beanie! 🎉
