---

THE BEGINNER-FRIENDLY SHOPPING LIST

For your first project (simple scarf), you need:

🧶 YARN:
• Weight: "Worsted" or "Aran" (medium thickness)
• Yardage: ~200 yards (enough for a scarf)
• Fiber: 100% acrylic (cheap, forgiving, washable)
• Brands to look for:
  - Red Heart Super Saver ($2-4)
  - Lion Brand Vanna's Choice ($2-3)
  - Caron Simply Soft ($3-4)
  
Why acrylic? It's beginner-friendly:
✓ Affordable (mistakes aren't expensive)
✓ Forgiving (tight stitches work out)
✓ Easy to frog (undo and start over)

🪡 NEEDLES:
• Size: US 8-10 (match your yarn)
• Material: Bamboo (grippy, won't slip)
• Type: Straight needles (easiest for beginners)
• Buy a SET ($5-10 for multiple sizes)

📍 WHERE TO BUY:
- Local yarn store (best! they'll help you pick)

- Amazon (Takli brand, highly rated)

PRO TIP: The Finger Flick Method

If you're struggling with traditional casting on, try this:

Instead of wrapping yarn around needles, flick it with your finger:
• Hold needle in right hand
• Wrap yarn around your LEFT hand fingers
• Flick it onto the needle
• Tighten gently

It's weird, but TONS of beginners find it easier.

TODAY'S PRO TIP:

One thing that helps beginners TONS:

CONSISTENCY IN TENSION

What does that mean?

Your cast-on stitches should be about the same tightness.

Not too loose (they'll fall off the needle).
Not too tight (you'll hurt your hand).

Just... even-ish.

How to fix:
1. Cast on slowly (you're not in a race)
2. After each stitch, loosen your yarn slightly
3. Pull each loop onto the needle gently
4. Breathe 😄

It won't be perfect.
It DOESN'T NEED TO BE.

Even wonky cast-ons work fine once you start knitting.
