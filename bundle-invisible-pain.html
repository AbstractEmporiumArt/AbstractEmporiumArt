    🌿 Therapeutic Coloring for Trauma & Pain

    These techniques are used by art therapists worldwide. No artistic skill required - just willingness to feel.

    🌑 Shadow Work

    What: Leave some areas intentionally dark or uncolored

    Why: Not everything needs to be "fixed" or "bright." Honor the darkness.

    Use for: Accepting what you can't change yet

    ✨ Highlight Healing

    What: Use metallic or gel pens to add bright spots to dark areas

    Why: Symbolizes hope emerging from pain

    Use for: Finding light in difficult times

    💪 Pressure Release

    What: Vary coloring pressure - hard for anger, gentle for sadness

    Why: Physical release of stored emotions

    Use for: When you need to let it OUT

    🌊 Gradient Healing

    What: Color from dark to light across the page

    Why: Visually represents the healing journey

    Use for: Tracking your progress over time
