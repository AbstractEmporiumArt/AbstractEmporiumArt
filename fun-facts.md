# Fun Facts — Z3nw1ck / AE (Casual / Interaction)
Not sales pitches — just random tidbits for social posts, stories, or conversation starters.
Created 2026-09-21.

---

1. Soy wax is water-soluble. Spill it? Soap + water cleans it up. Paraffin? That needs a scraper and a prayer.

2. A wax melt releases scent faster than a candle because there's no wick blocking the surface. Same soy wax, more surface = quicker scent. One melt = ~6-8 hours of smell.

3. The "Monkey Farts" scent contains zero actual banana. It's a synthetic fragrance blend — chemistry pretending to be a fruit. The real ingredient is your imagination.

4. Soy candles have "memory." First burn for at least 2 hours (full melt pool) or it tunnels forever. The wax remembers the first date. Don't ghost it.

5. Incense sticks burn ~45-60 minutes. The AE 20-pack tins aren't loose sticks — they're hand-batched, tied bundles in tins. If someone asks for "just one stick," that's like asking for half a sandwich.

6. Premium scents (Monkey Farts, Bourbon Sandalwood) use a heavier fragrance oil load than classic. That's why a 5oz premium candle costs $16 vs $10 classic — more FO per ounce, stronger scent throw, same soy base.

7. Hand-poured soy wax takes ~3 days to fully cure. The label says "refill price" because the tin is reusable — pour a new melt in, save the packaging. The wax doesn't judge.
