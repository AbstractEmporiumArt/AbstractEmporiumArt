## 📸 PHOTOGRAPHY SETUP (Use for ALL Images)

### Equipment Needed:
- Smartphone camera (iPhone/Android - good enough!)
- Natural lighting (near window) OR ring light
- Clean white/cream surface (table, cutting board, poster board)
- Optional: Small tripod or phone stand

### Standard Shot Setup:
- **Angle:** Birds-eye view (camera directly above hands) OR 45-degree angle
- **Background:** Clean, uncluttered, light-colored surface
- **Hands:** Always in frame showing technique
- **Yarn color:** Use contrasting colors (pink, teal, purple) so stitches are visible
- **Focus:** Sharp focus on needles/stitches, slight blur on background
- **Lighting:** Bright, even light (no harsh shadows)

### Photo Specs:
- **Resolution:** 1200x800px minimum (can crop from larger)
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Style:** Consistent across all photos (same background, same lighting)

## 🎬 CREATING THE FINAL PDFS

### Layout Each Pattern Like This:

**Page 1: Cover**
- Pattern name
- Difficulty level
- Photo of finished project
- Quick stats (time, cost, sizing)

**Page 2: Materials & Overview**
- Materials list with photos
- Skill overview
- Abbreviations key
- Sizing/measurement charts

**Pages 3-8: Step-by-Step Photos**
- 2-3 large photos per page
- Clear captions under each photo
- Arrow annotations where needed
- Step numbers

**Last Page: Troubleshooting & Tips**
- Common mistakes to avoid
- FAQ section
- Photo showing correct vs incorrect technique
- Link to video tutorial (if you make one later)

### Tools to Create PDFs:
- **Canva:** Easiest (free templates)
- **InDesign:** Professional (if you have it)
- **PowerPoint → PDF:** Simple and effective
- **Google Slides → PDF:** Free and collaborative
