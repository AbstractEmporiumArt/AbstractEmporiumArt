    Frequently Asked Questions

    How often can I contribute to the Community Canvas?

    You can add one contribution per day. This ensures everyone gets a chance to participate in our collaborative masterpiece!

    Can I download my generated patterns for free?

    Yes! PNG downloads are free. For PDF bundles with materials guides and expert tips, we offer affordable paid tiers.

    Where can I buy your artwork?

    You can find our art on ArtPal (digital), Fine Art America (prints), and The HUG (NFTs). Visit our Shop page for direct links.

    Can I collaborate with Abstract Emporium?

    We love collaborations! Reach out to us via email or contact form to discuss partnership opportunities.

    How do you use my email if I sign up?

    We only use your email for updates about our features, new artworks, and special offers. We never share your data.

    Is the Pattern Generator mobile-friendly?

    Yes! All our tools are optimized for desktop and mobile. Upload or select artwork on any device.
