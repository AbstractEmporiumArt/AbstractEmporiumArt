Welcome to Abstract Emporium

Abstract Emporium Art brings original, creative abstract artwork to art enthusiasts and collectors worldwide. Based in Thunder Bay, Ontario — a disability-owned small business — we ship handmade Z3NW1CK candles, wax melts & incense across Canada and beyond. Accessible, inclusive, made with care.

From digital collectibles to fine art prints, we offer diverse artistic expressions across multiple platforms.
