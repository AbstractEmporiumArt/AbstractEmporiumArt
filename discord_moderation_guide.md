# Discord Moderation Guidelines for 3mpwrapp

## Server Structure
The following Discord channels are set up for the 3mpwrapp team:

## #general
- General announcements and discussions
- Non-urgent questions and comments

## #standup (Daily Standup)
- Daily standup at 9:00 AM MTL time
- 15-minute duration
- Attendance tracked
- Agenda: What you did yesterday, what you're working on, blockers

## #standup-results (Results Channel)
- Daily standup results
- Action items and follow-ups
- Blockers and issues identified

## #beta-testing (Beta Testing)
- Beta test feedback and reports
- Feature testing results
- Bug reports and submissions

## #help (Help Desk)
- General questions and support
- Ticket submissions
- Guidance and resources

## #decisions (Important Decisions)
- Important decisions documented here
- Voting results and outcomes
- Action items and follow-ups

## Moderation Guidelines

### 1. ✅ Allowed Content
- Constructive feedback and suggestions
- Beta test results and feedback
- Questions and help requests
- Announcements and updates
- Positive feedback and recognition

### 2. ❌ Not Allowed
- Harassment or personal attacks
- Spam or irrelevant content
- Sharing confidential information
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Promotion of external products/services without approval

### 3. Moderation Actions
- **Mute**: 24-hour mute for first offense
- **Kick**: For repeated violations
- **Ban**: For severe or repeated violations
- **Report**: Use Discord's report function for serious issues

### 4. Moderation Tools
- **Hermes AI Moderation**: AI-assisted moderation
- **Human Moderation**: Lissa and designated moderators
- **Escalation**: Contact Lissa for serious issues

### 5. Moderation Workflow
1. Review new messages in #general and #standup
2. Flag concerning content for Lissa review
3. Respond to questions and support requests
4. Document decisions in #decisions channel
5. Escalate serious issues to Lissa

### 6. Moderator Responsibilities
- Review messages during standup hours
- Flag concerning content promptly
- Document decisions in #decisions channel
- Escalate serious issues to Lissa
- Maintain community guidelines

### 7. Escalation Protocol
- **Level 1**: Handle directly (moderator)
- **Level 2**: Contact Lissa (lissa@3mpwrapp.ca)
- **Level 3**: Emergency - contact Lissa immediately

### 8. Discord Roles
- **Admin**: Full moderation capabilities
- **Moderator**: Basic moderation capabilities
- **Member**: Standard participant

### 9. Moderation best practices:
- Be consistent in applying guidelines
- Document all moderation decisions
- Escalate uncertain cases to Lissa
- Maintain confidentiality of sensitive information
- Respond to messages within 24 hours
- Keep discussions focused and on-topic

### 10. Moderation Tools:
- Discord's built-in moderation features
- Hermes AI assistance for content flagging
- Manual review and documentation
- Regular moderation reviews (weekly)
