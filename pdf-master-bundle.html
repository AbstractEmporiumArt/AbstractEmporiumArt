Your 10+ Video Course Modules

This is a complete knitting education. Watch modules in any order, pause to practice, rewatch as needed.

📖 Module 1: Knitting Fundamentals (25 min)

The absolute basics. Holding needles, yarn tension, basic stitches. Perfect refresher even if experienced.

🧵 Module 2: Yarn & Fiber Science (30 min)

All fiber types (wool, alpaca, cotton, synthetic). Weights, yardage, care instructions. How to choose yarn.

✳️ Module 3: 20 Essential Stitches (45 min)

Beyond knit and purl. Increases, decreases, twisted stitches, yarn overs. Master the toolkit.

🔧 Module 4: Fixing Any Mistake (35 min)

Dropped stitches, laddering down, unraveling sections, fixing tension issues. Nothing is unfixable.

✨ Module 5: Cable Stitches Mastery (30 min)

All cable variations. Left, right, centered cables. Cable charts. Patterns combining cables.

🎨 Module 6: Color Work (35 min)

Stranding (carrying yarn). Fair isle patterns. Managing two colors. Intarsia basics.

👗 Module 7: Garment Construction (40 min)

Shaping sleeves. Necklines. Increases and decreases. Seaming techniques. Perfect fit.

✨ Module 8: Lace & Advanced Techniques (35 min)

Yarn overs. Lace charts. Openwork patterns. Blocking. Creating ethereal fabrics.

📐 Module 9: Pattern Reading & Writing (40 min)

Understanding complex patterns. Reading charts. Stitch symbols. Creating your own patterns.

🎯 Module 10: Pattern Design Course (60 min)

How to design your own patterns from scratch. Custom sweaters, hats, blankets for your style.

🏆 Module 11: Mastery & Professional Techniques (45 min)

Advanced finishing. Professional blocking. Yarn substitution. Troubleshooting complex patterns.

🌟 Module 12: Your Knitting Business (Optional, 30 min)

For those interested: selling knitted items, pricing, building a portfolio, craft show tips.

Your Path to Mastery

Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)

Patterns 1-2 (Dishcloth, Scarf). Review fundamentals. Build muscle memory.

Watch Modules 1-3. Get comfortable with basic techniques.

Share work in community. Get feedback and encouragement.

Phase 2: Building (Weeks 5-12)

Patterns 3-4 (Blanket, Sweater). Learn cables and shaping.

Watch Modules 4-6. Cables, fixing mistakes, color work.

Start designing simple modifications of existing patterns.

Phase 3: Advanced (Weeks 13-24)

Patterns 5-6 (Shawl, Cardigan). Master advanced techniques.

Watch Modules 7-9. Garments, lace, pattern reading.

Start designing your first original small pattern.

Phase 4: Mastery (Months 7+)

Patterns 7-8 (Intarsia, Your Design). Complete major projects.

Watch Modules 10-11. Pattern design mastery.

Design patterns confidently. Create gifts and portfolio pieces.

Help others in community. Give back.
