# Abstract Emporium: Bundle-First Architecture & Conversion Strategy
**Transforming into a Beginner-Friendly, Automated Knitting Digital Business**

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This document outlines the complete transformation of Abstract Emporium from an art gallery website into a **beginner-first, bundle-driven, fully-automated knitting digital business** capable of generating passive income with minimal human input.

**Core Strategy:**
- **Primary Revenue Driver:** Bundles (Free → Beginner → Starter → Master)
- **Target Audience:** Absolute beginners ("never knitted before")
- **Automation Level:** 90%+ (AI agent, email sequences, daily social posts)
- **Tone:** Calm, encouraging, confidence-building, non-intimidating
- **Scalability:** Easy pattern/bundle addition with auto-updating systems
