PATTERN 1: Ribbed Baby Blanket

Difficulty: Beginner+
Time: 8-10 hours
Finished Size: 24" x 30"
Yarn: 1200 yards worsted weight

What You'll Learn

  • Ribbed stitches (knit-purl combinations)
  • Larger projects
  • Consistent tension across many rows

Materials

  • US 9-10 needles
  • Worsted weight yarn, 1200 yards

Pattern

Cast on: 40 stitches

Row 1: K2 (knit 2), P2 (purl 2), repeat across

Row 2: P2, K2, repeat across

Repeat Rows 1-2 until piece measures 30 inches long

Bind off in ribbed pattern

Notes

  • Ribbed patterns look fancy but are just alternating knit and purl
  • Keep tension consistent. Ribbed stitches are forgiving if tension varies slightly
  • This blanket makes an amazing newborn gift!

PATTERN 2: Cable Knit Socks

Difficulty: Intermediate
Time: 6-8 hours
Finished Size: Adult small/medium
Yarn: 400 yards fingering weight

What You'll Learn

  • Cable stitches (the fun twist!)
  • Small circumference knitting
  • Shaping (toe and heel)

Cable Stitch Explained

A cable is made by temporarily moving stitches to a cable needle, knitting the next stitches, then knitting the stitches from the cable needle.

Example: Cable 4 front means:

  1. Slip 2 stitches to cable needle
  2. Knit 2 stitches from main needle
  3. Knit 2 stitches from cable needle
  4. Result: A twisted "cable" pattern

Materials

  • US 2-3 circular needles (small circumference)
  • Fingering weight yarn, 400 yards
  • Cable needle

Basic Sock Pattern

Cast on: 60 stitches (on circular needle)

Leg (1.5 inches): Work ribbed pattern

Foot section (4 inches): Cables every 8 stitches

Heel: Work back and forth on 30 stitches

Foot to toe: Continue cables

Toe: Decrease to point, bind off

Note: Sock knitting has a learning curve. That's okay! Watch the video twice if you need to.
