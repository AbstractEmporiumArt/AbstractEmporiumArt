Where It All Began — 12 Years of Creativity

It started with little bookmarks as a kid — wacky colours and drawings. Then life got in the way. In my late 20s, one afternoon I grabbed paint and made a glorious mess. That rabbit hole of fluid acrylics turned into a decade of playing with light and texture. Those first paintings are still with me. Today the same hands that make those early pours now pour candles, knit slippers, and keep creating. Read the full story on About →

From sketchbook kid to handmade business — the thread is the same: making things by hand, no AI, no mass production.
