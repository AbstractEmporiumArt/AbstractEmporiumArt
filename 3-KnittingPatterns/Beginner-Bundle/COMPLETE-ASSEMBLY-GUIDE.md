```
Circular needles have two needle tips connected
by a flexible cable. Stitches rest on the cable
```

## 📄 PAGE 4 - JOINING IN THE ROUND

**Image 1:** `Pattern1ChunkyInfinityScarfCircularPhoto4JoiningintheRoundSetupAbstractEmporium.png`

**Caption:**
```
STEP 3: Check for Twists

Before joining, make sure ALL stitches face
the same direction. The cast-on edge should
form a straight line around the cable, not
twisted.
```

**Image 2:** `Pattern1ChunkyInfinityScarfCircularPhoto5JoiningintheRoundConnectionAbstractEmporium.png`

```
STEP 4: Join in the Round

1. Bring both needle tips together
2. Place stitch marker on right needle
3. Knit the first stitch to join the circle
4. Continue knitting around and around

The stitch marker shows the beginning of each round!
```

```
• Knit every stitch
```

```
STEP 2: Seed Stitch Pattern

Seed stitch alternates knit and purl stitches:
Row 1: *K1, P1* repeat to end
Row 2: *P1, K1* repeat to end

This creates a bumpy, textured fabric that
lays flat and looks the same on both sides!
```

---

## 📄 PAGE 4 - LEARNING PURL STITCH

**Image 1:** `Pattern3CozyHeadbandEarWarmerPhoto4PurlStitchTutorialStep1AbstractEmporium.png`

**Caption:**
```
PURL STITCH - Step 1: Insert Needle

Unlike knit stitch (front to back),
purl stitch goes BACK TO FRONT

1. Bring working yarn to FRONT of work
2. Insert right needle from back to front
   (right to left through stitch)
3. Needles form a different angle than knit
```

**Image 2:** `Pattern3CozyHeadbandEarWarmerPhoto5PurlStitchTutorialStep 2AbstractEmporium.png`

**Caption:**
```
PURL STITCH - Step 2: Wrap & Complete

1. Wrap yarn around right needle CLOCKWISE
   (opposite direction from knit!)
2. Pull wrapped yarn back through stitch
3. Slide old stitch off left needle

New purl stitch on right needle!

REMEMBER: Yarn always stays in FRONT for purl.
```

---

## 📄 PAGE 5 - WORKING THE PATTERN

**Image 1:** `Pattern3CozyHeadbandEarWarmerPhoto6SeedStitchPatternFormingAbstractEmporium.png`

**Caption:**
```
STEP 3: Seed Stitch Texture

After a few rows, you'll see the bumpy
texture forming. Every stitch looks like
a little "seed" or bump!

Row 1: K1, P1, K1, P1, K1, P1... (to end)
Row 2: P1, K1, P1, K1, P1, K1... (to end)

TIP: "Knit the purls, purl the knits"
If stitch looks like a V (knit), purl it.
If stitch looks like a bump (purl), knit it.
```

**Image 2:** `Pattern3CozyHeadbandEarWarmerPhoto7ContinuingtoLengthAbstractEmporium.png`

```
STEP 4: Continue to 18 Inches
```

## 📄 PAGE 4 - DOUBLE-POINTED NEEDLES TUTORIAL

**Image:** `Pattern5SimpleSlippersPhoto4DoublePointedNeedlesTutorialAbstractEmporium.png`

**Caption:**
```
HOW TO USE DPNs

Setup:
• 3 needles hold stitches (triangle formation)
• 4th needle is empty (working needle)

To knit:
1. Work stitches from needle 1 onto empty needle
2. Needle 1 becomes empty (new working needle)
3. Move to needle 2, work those stitches
4. Continue rotating around the triangle

TIPS:
• Keep yarn snug between needles (prevent gaps)
• Points can feel awkward at first - normal!
• Focus on one needle at a time
• Stitches won't fall off if you go slowly
```

---

## 📄 PAGE 5 - SHAPING THE TOE

```
STEP 3: Increase for Toe Shape

Round 2: *K1, M1 (make 1 increase)* repeat
Result: 48 stitches (16 per needle)

Continue increasing every other round until
toe measures 2 inches long.

M1 (Make 1):
Pick up bar between stitches with left needle,
knit through back loop. Creates invisible increase!
```

**Image 2:** `Pattern5SimpleSlippersPhoto8ShortRowHeelWrappingStitchesAbstractEmporium.png`

**Caption:**
```
SHORT ROW TECHNIQUE

Row 1: K11, W&T (wrap & turn)
- Slip next stitch
- Bring yarn to front
- Slip stitch back
- Turn work (now working wrong side)

Row 2: P10, W&T

Continue working fewer stitches each row,
creating a curve.

The wraps prevent holes - you'll knit them
together with stitches later!
```
