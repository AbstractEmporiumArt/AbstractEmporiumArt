## 📊 CHATBOT ANALYTICS TO TRACK

**Conversation Metrics:**
- Most common first question
- Average messages before purchase
- Top objections encountered
- Conversion rate: chatbot engagement → purchase

**Optimization Opportunities:**
- Which buttons get clicked most?
- Where do users drop off?
- What questions aren't answered well?

**A/B Tests:**
- Opening message variations
- Button text ("Show me" vs "Learn more")
- Response timing (immediate vs 1-second delay)
