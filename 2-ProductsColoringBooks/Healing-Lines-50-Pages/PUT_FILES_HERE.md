# 📁 WHERE TO PUT YOUR GRAPHICS

## PUT YOUR FILES HERE:

### Main PDF:
- `healing-lines-coloring-book.pdf` ← Your 50-page PDF goes here (300 DPI, 8.5x11")

### Cover Image:
- `cover.jpg` ← Product thumbnail (1500x1500px square, shows calming flowing designs)

### Preview Images (for marketing):
Put 3-5 sample pages in `preview-images/` folder:
- `page-01-preview.jpg` (1200x1500px, 72 DPI)
- `page-25-preview.jpg`
- `page-45-preview.jpg`

These previews will be used for:
- Etsy listing photos
- Pinterest pins
- Mindfulness/relaxation groups
- Stress relief communities

## Example:
```
Healing-Lines-50-Pages/
├── healing-lines-coloring-book.pdf  ← MAIN PRODUCT
├── cover.jpg                         ← PRODUCT THUMBNAIL
└── preview-images/
    ├── page-01-preview.jpg           ← SAMPLE 1
    ├── page-25-preview.jpg           ← SAMPLE 2
    └── page-45-preview.jpg           ← SAMPLE 3
```

Once you add these files, the product page will automatically link to them!
